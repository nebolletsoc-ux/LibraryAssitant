MyNextRead — Privacy Policy

Last updated 07 September 2026

What we store

  • Account: your username and a salted password hash. No email address is required to sign up.
  • Optional email: only if you add one, for "book became available" alerts and the weekly digest you opt into.
  • Your reading list: the titles and (when present) ISBNs you import or add, so the service can check availability for you.
  • Library preferences: which library catalogs you choose to check.

What we don't store or do

  • We don't sell, rent, or share your data with any third party.
  • We don't read your reading history or track you across other sites. The only cookie is a small session cookie (plus a CSRF token cookie) — no advertising, analytics, or fingerprinting.
  • We don't access your library accounts. Borrow links go to the library's own website, where the library's own privacy policy applies.

How data is processed

To check availability, the app sends public catalog searches containing your book titles to the selected libraries' publicly indexed sites (OverDrive/Libby, Bibliocommons, Hoopla) and to Open Library for book metadata. Those sites' privacy policies apply to those requests.

Data retention and deletion

Deleting your account (Settings → Delete my account) permanently removes your account, library preferences, and reading-list rows, including any book records not shared with other accounts. Backups created automatically by the hosting provider may retain encrypted copies for a short time.

Security

Traffic is served over HTTPS. Passwords are stored as salted hashes and the session cookie is marked HttpOnly, SameSite, and Secure. Logins are rate-limited and brute-force-locked to protect accounts.

Legal bases

We process your data to provide the service you asked for (performance of contract) and to keep it reliable and secure (legitimate interest).

Contact

Questions or data-deletion requests: open an issue at github.com/nebolletsoc-ux/LibraryAssitant.
