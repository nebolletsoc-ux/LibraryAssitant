What the service does

MyNextRead stores your personal to-read list, checks several public library catalogs (OverDrive/Libby, Bibliocommons, Hoopla) for what is available, and links you out to those libraries to borrow. It does not host, reproduce, or transmit copyrighted books, audiobooks, or other media.

Acceptable use

  • Registering fake accounts, impersonating others, or attempting to disrupt the service may result in account termination.
  • Do not attempt to gain access to accounts that are not yours.
